# Product Source of Truth

## Vision

Create a persistent private world that feels like a living social network populated by believable AI characters. The player reads a feed, posts, replies, follows characters, chats privately, forms friendships and rivalries, dates, marries, breaks up, and observes careers, trends, and life events evolve over time.

## User model

- Many real accounts may eventually exist.
- Every account plays alone.
- Real accounts never share a world.
- Real users never see, message, follow, or influence one another.
- Each account may eventually own multiple private worlds.
- Version 1 may expose only one world per account.

## Core gameplay loop

1. Open the app.
2. Read new feed posts, messages, notifications, and world summary.
3. Interact with AI characters.
4. Game systems update relationships, memories, reputation, and trends.
5. Simulation advances while the app is active or through catch-up processing.
6. Return later and discover changes.

## Product success criteria

The world should feel persistent, character behaviour should be understandable from personality and history, and the player should observe emergent stories without AI directly controlling numerical game mechanics.
